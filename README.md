# Oregon Water Well Data — County Statistics

County-level summary statistics for documented water wells in Oregon, computed from the
official OWRD well database: median drilled depth, static water level, tested yield,
and modeled drilling cost per county. See [`or-water-well-county-statistics.csv`](./or-water-well-county-statistics.csv).

Compiled and maintained by **[Oregon Well Data](https://oregonwelldata.com)** — free per-address well lookups and
full methodology at [oregonwelldata.com](https://oregonwelldata.com). Licensed CC-BY-4.0. Aggregate statistics only;
individual well records are not included.
